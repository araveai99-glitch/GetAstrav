## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a focused, high-clarity visual language for enterprise goal orchestration, company-wide execution trees, and executive command centers. Built to replace sterile corporate software with warm authority, it merges crisp enterprise structural clarity with an organic warm-palette philosophy. 

### Brand Personality & Core Principles
- **Unwavering Architectural Precision:** Every element conveys mathematical rigor, operational certainty, and nested governance.
- **Warm Executive Warmth:** Replaces clinical, cold slate interfaces with warm terracotta undertones, radiant earthen tones, and luminous peach hues without losing analytical authority.
- **High-Information Density with Visual Breathing Room:** Complex 7-level structural cascades (Vision down to Individual OKRs and Key Results) remain readable, structured, and visually digestible.
- **Deterministic Actionability:** States, progress vectors, automated rollups, and permissions feel physical, tactile, and instantly parseable.

### Aesthetic Direction
A modern synthesis of **Warm Structural Modernism** and **Tactile Analytical Glass**:
- Pristine off-white foundation surfaces (`#ffffff`, `#fffaf5`, `#fef5ee`) layered with razor-thin warm-tinted structural borders.
- Absolute exclusion of cold neutrals (slate, cool grays, midnight blues, synthetic pitch blacks) in favor of deep espresso and roasted terracotta typographics.
- High-contrast visual focus driven by radiant enterprise orange, validated by deep forest jade, and illuminated by amber badges.

## Colors

The color architecture eliminates cool grays, slate, pure obsidian, navy, and clinical blues. Depth, text fidelity, and surface tiers rely entirely on an espresso-to-terracotta spectrum anchored by warm peach-cast light values.

### Core Swatches & Semantics
- **Brand Action Primary (`#f15e1c`):** High-energy primary orange. Dictates directional calls-to-action, primary tree anchors, critical path highlights, and focal KPI markers.
- **System Validated Secondary (`#2e936f`):** Crisp botanical emerald. Reserved strictly for forward velocity, target completions, positive delta variances, health scores, and operational uptime.
- **Alert & Attention Amber (`#fab60a`):** Warm golden yellow. Denotes cautionary metrics, pending approvals, intermediate dependencies, and high-visibility tier badges.
- **Light Accenting Ray (`#ffec69`):** Soft luminous yellow. Used for subtle metric indicator backdrops, active multi-select highlights, and focus halos.
- **Warm Canvas Buffer (`#f7d7b0`):** Soft foundational peach. Serves as a tertiary container base, dividing rule, or soft indicator frame.

### Typographic Tonal Scale (Espresso & Terracotta)
- **Text Heavy / Title Canvas (`#2a1a14`):** Deep roasted espresso. Delivers razor contrast without the harsh artificial coldness of #000000.
- **Text Medium / Structural Narrative (`#3d261e`):** Balanced dark terracotta. Used for subheads, long-form system briefs, and secondary metric labels.
- **Text Muted / Operational Metadata (`#4a332a`):** Soft earthen espresso. Dedicated to column labels, microcopy, breadcrumbs, timestamp meta, and subtle borders.

### Surface Canvas Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas (`#ffffff`):** Pure pristine white for hero blocks and high-focus content islands.
- **Primary Ambient Surface (`#fffaf5`):** Ultra-soft warm peach-white for primary section backdrops.
- **Elevated Interactive Tier (`#fef5ee`):** Warmer inner container fill, nesting child goals and metric card bases.
- **Recessed / Secondary Surface (`#fdf8f4`):** Subtle depth tier for tree branches, sidebars, and sticky rollup headers.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all breakpoints. Its geometric clarity and humanized curves provide high legibility in dense multi-tiered data tables while retaining punch and commanding executive scale in marketing and command-center hero contexts.

### Typographic Roles & Execution
- **Display Hero & H1 / H2:** Emphasize dramatic optical authority. Kerning is pulled tight (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to anchor the value proposition of FounderOS. The color is anchored directly in Deep Espresso (`#2a1a14`).
- **Body & Structural Text:** Set with open line heights (`1.55` - `1.65`) in Dark Terracotta (`#3d261e`) to guarantee zero optical strain when scanning architectural organizational maps or operational treatises.
- **Analytical & System Data (`data-mono` / `label-caps`):** Used for rollups, percentages, multi-tier RBAC role definitions, and 7-level OKR taxonomy identifiers. All uppercase tracking is boosted to `+0.08em` for distinct semantic clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses an **Adaptive 12-Column Hierarchical Grid** calibrated for dense data visualization, wide executive dashboards, and narrative conversion funnels.

### Grid Hierarchy & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px - 1440px+):** 12-column layout. Max content canvas: 1320px centered. Gutter: 24px (`1.5rem`). Canvas Margins: 64px (`4rem`).
- **Tablet / Small Laptop (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Gutters: 20px (`1.25rem`). Margins: 32px (`2rem`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Gutters: 16px (`1rem`). Margins: 20px (`1.25rem`). Dual-mode analytics collapse from split-pane to vertically stacked toggle tabs.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Micro-Scale (0.25rem - 0.5rem):** Strict association between metric labels, progress percentage figures, and small tier badges.
- **Container Scale (1rem - 1.5rem):** Standard inner padding for cards, RBAC matrix modules, and goal tree child nodes.
- **Structural Scale (2.5rem - 5rem):** Space between macro components, separating distinct hierarchy levels, product modules, and narrative blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids heavy synthetic drop shadows or cold blue-gray ambient occlusion. Instead, surfaces create depth via **Layered Warm Tonal Shells** combined with low-opacity warm-amber diffuse halos.

### Surface Depth Layers
- **Tier 0 (Root Canvas):** `#ffffff` or `#fffaf5`. Flattest baseline with zero elevation.
- **Tier 1 (Base Cards & Tree Nodes):** Surface fill `#ffffff` with a subtle hairline perimeter: `1px solid rgba(74, 51, 42, 0.08)`. Shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(42, 26, 20, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Modules & Hover Elements):** Surface fill `#ffffff` with accent-tinted boundaries. Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(241, 94, 28, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(42, 26, 20, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Command Modals & Flyouts):** Surface fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.96)` with backdrop blur `12px`. Hairline: `1px solid rgba(247, 215, 176, 0.60)`. Shadow: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(42, 26, 20, 0.12), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(241, 94, 28, 0.1)`.

### Luminous Ambient Flares
In hero sections and key analytical milestones, soft radial glows provide natural visual focus:
- **Primary Glow:** `radial-gradient(circle at center, rgba(241, 94, 28, 0.12) 0%, rgba(254, 245, 238, 0) 70%)`
- **Validation Glow:** `radial-gradient(circle at center, rgba(46, 147, 111, 0.12) 0%, rgba(254, 245, 238, 0) 70%)`

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 2 (Rounded)** geometric rules. Corners are softened to project high polish and approachability, yet tightly bounded to maintain the structural credibility demanded by mission-critical enterprise workflows.

### Radius Assignments
- **Micro UI (Badges, Checkboxes, Micro Chips):** `0.375rem` (6px).
- **Controls & Input Fields (Buttons, Selectors, Inputs):** `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Cards, Panels & Data Cards:** `1rem` (16px) for standard modules; `1.25rem` (20px) for high-level command center cards.
- **Outer Hero Frames & App Mockup Enclosures:** `1.5rem` (24px) with continuous, squircle-like corner curves.
- **Pills / Status Chips:** Pill shape (`9999px`) reserved specifically for single-attribute indicators, execution phase tags, and progress badges.

## Components

### 1. Primary Action Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Solid background `#f15e1c`, text `#ffffff`, font weight `600`, border-radius `0.5rem`. Subtle box-shadow: `0 2px 8px rgba(241, 94, 28, 0.28)`. Hover state transitions to `#d94e10` with elevation lift.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid rgba(74, 51, 42, 0.16)`, text `#2a1a14`. Hover state: background `#fef5ee`, border-color `#f15e1c`, text `#f15e1c`.
- **Tertiary Executive Button:** Background `transparent`, text `#3d261e` with inline chevron, border-bottom `1px solid transparent`. Hover: border-color `#f15e1c`, text `#f15e1c`.

### 2. Status Chips & Badges
- **Success / Validated Target:** Background `rgba(46, 147, 111, 0.12)`, text `#2e936f`, border `1px solid rgba(46, 147, 111, 0.25)`.
- **Attention / Mid-Dependency:** Background `rgba(250, 182, 10, 0.14)`, text `#2a1a14`, border `1px solid rgba(250, 182, 10, 0.35)`.
- **Active Focus / Strategic Phase:** Background `rgba(241, 94, 28, 0.10)`, text `#f15e1c`, border `1px solid rgba(241, 94, 28, 0.24)`.

### 3. Cards & Command Modules
- Enterprise card containers are constructed with pure white `#ffffff` or warm-tint `#fffaf5` surface fills.
- Outlines use hairline warm boundaries (`1px solid rgba(74, 51, 42, 0.08)`).
- Every card includes a distinct header zone with an espresso title (`#2a1a14`), an operational status pill, and a soft divider in `#f7d7b0` at 40% opacity.

### 4. Progress Visualizations & Mathematical Rollups
- **Track Rail:** Height `6px` or `8px`, background `rgba(74, 51, 42, 0.06)`, border-radius `9999px`.
- **Active Progress Fill:** Gradient progression from `#f15e1c` to `#fab60a`, or solid `#2e936f` for locked 100% completion metrics.
- **Mathematical Rollup Nodes:** Circular nodes along tree branches displaying parent contribution percentages (e.g., `+18.4% to Core ARR Goal`), set in `#2e936f` typography inside an elevated `#ffffff` container.

### 5. Input Fields & Select Controls
- Input surfaces: `#ffffff` with a warm structural border: `1px solid rgba(74, 51, 42, 0.15)`.
- Placeholder text: `#4a332a` at 60% opacity. Focus state applies a crisp perimeter: `1.5px solid #f15e1c` and a subtle halo of `0 0 0 3px rgba(241, 94, 28, 0.15)`. No harsh black or cool gray outlines.

### 6. Interactive Command Tabs & Switchers
- Segmented container with `#fef5ee` fill and `0.5rem` radius.
- Inactive tabs: text `#4a332a`, no shadow, smooth hover shift to `#2a1a14`.
- Active tab: solid `#ffffff` pill, text `#2a1a14`, subtle shadow `0 2px 8px rgba(42, 26, 20, 0.06)`, border `1px solid rgba(74, 51, 42, 0.06)`.

### 7. Specialized 7-Level Hierarchy & RBAC Components
- **Tree Hierarchy Connectors:** `1.5px` connecting spine lines rendered in `rgba(74, 51, 42, 0.14)` linking strategic objectives down to leaf tasks.
- **RBAC Matrix Badges:** Tiered role access badges (Owner, Executive, Orchestrator, Contributor) defined with explicit warm amber or emerald indicators to ensure immediate visual governance.
